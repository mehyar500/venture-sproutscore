FAQ

Fair questions.

Where does the data come from?

NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (DOHMH) child-care inspection records — public data. We show the "data as of" date on every result so you always know how fresh it is.

Is the free search really free?

Yes. Search any center, see its inspection counts, flagged count, standing vs the NYC average, and last inspection date — free, no account. The email capture is optional and gets you your result by email plus 3 tour questions.

What does the $19 report add?

Every flagged violation decoded from inspector code into plain English: what actually happened, how serious it is, whether the city recorded a correction, plus a severity summary, nearby-center comparison, exact tour questions, and printable result cards.

Does a flagged inspection mean the daycare is bad?

Not necessarily — and that's exactly why we decode. A missing first-aid item and an unsafe infant sleep setup are both "violations" in the raw data but mean very different things. The report separates critical from minor and shows what the city recorded as corrected.

What if my daycare isn't listed?

We cover licensed NYC daycares in the DOHMH dataset. If yours is missing, email team@mehyar.us and we'll look into it.

Not legal or childcare advice. SproutScore translates public NYC inspection records into plain English. It is an information service, not a recommendation for or against any childcare provider. Always tour in person and verify with the NYC DOHMH.
