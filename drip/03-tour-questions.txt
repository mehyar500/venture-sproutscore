Parents who've read the inspection file tour differently. Not
confrontational — just specific. Directors notice, and they answer
differently.

Three questions that work at any center:

1. "What is your staff-to-child ratio right now, in this room — and
   how do you cover breaks without breaking ratio?"
   (Ratios are the #1 thing inspectors cite. Watch whether the answer
   is instant or vague.)

2. "May I see your two most recent full inspection reports — not just
   the summary?"
   (Centers are used to this ask. Hesitation tells you plenty.)

3. "What changed the day after your last flagged inspection?"
   (Every center has a last inspection. This asks about follow-through,
   not perfection.)
