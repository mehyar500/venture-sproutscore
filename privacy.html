Privacy Policy

Last updated September 19, 2026.

What we collect

SproutScore is a product of MehyarSoft LLC. We collect the minimum needed to run the service:

  • Email address — when you ask for your free result by email or buy a report. Used to deliver your result, your receipt, and your private report link.
  • Daycare you looked up — the center ID and name attached to a purchase, so we can generate the right report.
  • Payment details — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. Ever.
  • We never share your email with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We never email you marketing without your opt-in.

Email and unsubscribe

If you opt in to emails (your free result, report updates), every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. Clicking it removes you immediately from SproutScore emails — no login, no confirmation hoops. You can also unsubscribe anytime at sproutscore.mehyar.us/unsubscribe.html.

Data sources

Inspection records come from the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (DOHMH), a public dataset. We show the "data as of" date on every result.

Your rights

Email team@mehyar.us to request a copy of your data or its deletion. We respond within 30 days.
